01

Methodology and second language acquisition

7

  • An A–Z of ELT ↗ Scott Thornbury

    short essays on key teaching and SLA concepts, from noticing and chunks to Dogme and fluency. Long reader debates under each post.

    scottthornbury.wordpress.com
  • The Language Gym ↗ Gianfranco Conti

    input-heavy, chunk-based teaching (the E.P.I. approach), sentence builders, and memory and listening research applied to the classroom.

    gianfrancoconti.com
  • Language Teacher Toolkit ↗ Steve Smith

    research-informed commentary on listening, vocabulary and Paul Nation’s principles, plus book reviews.

    frenchteachernet.blogspot.com
  • What do you think you’re doing? ↗ Geoff Jordan

    SLA-based critiques of teacher training and coursebook-led teaching, and the case for task-based language teaching.

    applingtesol.wordpress.com
  • Barefoot TEFL Teacher ↗ David Weller

    clear explainers on core concepts (comprehensible input, TBL vs TBLT, concept-checking questions, eliciting, pairwork).

    barefootteflteacher.com
  • Demand High ELT ↗ Jim Scrivener & Adrian Underhill

    pushing every learner to their next achievable step instead of settling for “task done”.

    demandhighelt.wordpress.com
  • Fluency First ELT ↗ Neil Anderson & Neil McCutcheon

    task-based speaking lessons and how to give feedback on emergent language.

    fluencyfirstelt.blog
